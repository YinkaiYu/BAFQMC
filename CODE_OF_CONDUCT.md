# Community conduct

BAFQMC welcomes researchers and developers from all backgrounds and levels of
experience. Treat people respectfully, discuss scientific and technical ideas
on their merits, and give appropriate credit for contributions.

Harassment, discriminatory language, personal attacks, threats, and sharing
someone's private information without permission are unacceptable. These
expectations apply to the repository's issues, pull requests, and other
project discussions, including contributions prepared with an agent.

Maintainers may edit or remove inappropriate content and restrict participation
when necessary. For a conduct concern, contact the repository maintainer
[@YinkaiYu](https://github.com/YinkaiYu) through a contact method listed on
their GitHub profile. Keep sensitive details out of public issues.
